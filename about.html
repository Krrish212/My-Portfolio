About

Early starts, long arguments, and someone who likes finishing what they start.

I'm Krishang — a Computer Science and AI student at the University of Bath, though I'd rather be judged on the rest of it. Most days start earlier than I'd like and involve a barbell, and I've come round to the idea that almost everything worth doing is boring and repetitive long before it's impressive. The gym taught me that well before any deadline did.

Outside that, I help run Bath's Hindu Society as Sanskaar Officer — weekly prayer sessions, cultural events, and a long-running campaign for a permanent Mandir on campus. I read a lot, the Gita most of all. I cook more ambitiously than my kitchen deserves, I travel whenever I can talk myself into it, and I'll take a proper argument over small talk every time. Last year I ran a half marathon for Andy's Man Club and raised over £500 doing it.
