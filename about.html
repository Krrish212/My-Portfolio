About

Student, builder, and someone who likes finishing what they start.

I'm a penultimate-year Computer Science and AI student at the University of Bath, currently holding a First Class standing. My path through three very different placements — AI/ML engineering at Accenture, portfolio risk analysis at Barclays, and product delivery at Dojo — has shaped what I actually want to do: build technology in financial services and asset management, where technical depth and clear business communication both matter.

Outside of internships, most of my time goes into applied ML projects I choose specifically because they force me to learn something new — reinforcement learning for trading, NLP from first principles, VR for accessibility. I care more about being able to explain why something works (or doesn't) than about chasing the flashiest result.
